The challenge

Orthodontic practices compete in one of the most crowded local search markets there is. When a parent searches "braces near me" or "Invisalign for teens," the practices that appear first capture the consultation — and every practice on the block is fighting for those same few spots.

Young Orthodontics needed more than scattered posting. They needed a connected system: search visibility that brought the right local patients in, content that answered the questions parents actually ask before booking, and a consistent brand across every touchpoint.

My approach

I started with research, not production. Before creating a single asset, I mapped the local competitive landscape, audited the practice's search presence, and analysed what prospective patients were actually searching for at each stage — from "how much do braces cost" through to "best orthodontist near me." That research shaped everything that followed.

Search & local visibility

A full SEO audit surfaced the technical and on-page gaps holding the site back, feeding a local strategy built on treatment-intent keywords that convert into consultations — not vanity terms with no booking intent.

Educational content

Orthodontics is a considered purchase, usually for a child. Content answered parents' real questions honestly — options, timelines, costs — earning rankings and building booking-level trust at the same time.

Social media & design

Consistent, on-brand content and graphic design across every platform, so the practice looked as polished and credible online as it does in person.

AI-powered production

AI-assisted workflows increased content volume and consistency without lowering the standard — more touchpoints, faster turnaround, same quality bar.

The insight that drove it

One community post earned 1,100+ views and 35 shares — outperforming every promotional post combined.

A Women's Only Self-Defense Seminar sponsorship announcement was the month's runaway performer. That finding reshaped the strategy: people share content that supports their community, not content that advertises a discount. Community-partnership posting was doubled going into the following month.
